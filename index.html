Massachusetts Schools of Recognition, 2026

All 56 recognized schools plotted by town. Color shows school level, and the outlined filters narrow by district, charter or vocational. Scroll or use the buttons to zoom, drag to pan, and select a school in the list to find it on the map.

Each pin is geocoded from the school's street address.

    Source list: 2026 Schools of Recognition as supplied. Street addresses come from school, district and directory pages, with Kittredge, Berkshire Arts and Technology, Kennedy Academy and Page Hilltop confirmed by you. Pins come from TomTom street-level geocoding of those addresses; where the geocoder matched a nearby number or ZIP, the tooltip says so. Grade spans come from school and directory pages; schools listed without a span (most high schools and junior/senior highs) are classified by name. None of the 56 is a K-8 school. Boundaries are from US Census cartographic data (us-atlas).
